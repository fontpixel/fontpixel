The character list files in `pipeline/opf/coverage/data/` marked
`license: MIT (CJK-character-count, © NFSL2001)` are taken from the standard
character lists compiled by the CJK-character-count project:

- Table of General Standard Chinese Characters (通用规范汉字表)
- List of Frequently Used Characters in Modern Chinese (现代汉语常用字表)
- List of Commonly Used Characters in Modern Chinese (现代汉语通用字表)
- Frequently Used Characters for Chinese Language Education in Compulsory Schooling (义务教育语文课程常用字表)
- Standard Glyph Table of Common Characters for Printing Ancient Books (古籍印刷通用字规范字形表)
- GB/T 12345
- IICore
- Hanyi / Founder simplified and traditional character lists (汉仪/方正简繁字表)
- Taiwan's Standard Form of Common and Less-Common National Characters (台湾常用/次常用国字标准字体表)
- List of Graphemes of Commonly-Used Chinese Characters, Hong Kong (香港常用字字形表)

## Unicode Character Database

The data in `pipeline/opf/coverage/data/ucd/` comes from the Unicode 17.0.0
Character Database (https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/ucd/) and is used
under the Unicode License v3 (https://www.unicode.org/license.txt).

## Codec-derived lists

The character lists marked `source: Python codecs` were generated by
enumerating the codecs of the Python standard library (gb2312, gbk, big5,
euc_jp, euc_jis_2004, euc_kr, cp437). They are factual data.
